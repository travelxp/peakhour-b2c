## c) Open owner decisions

**Settled 2026-11-16 (read from the database; dev and prod agree unless stated):**
- **The trial question is closed, and the wording is database-driven.** `cfg_platform_stage` is `coming_soon` with `signupMode` `waitlist_only`, and the Suite plan grants `trialDays` 14 in all 11 country pricing entries. The trial is real, but it starts when a person is approved. Every trial line now comes from `signupMode` and `suiteTrialDays` through `src/lib/trial-copy.ts` (`trialStart`): waitlist or invite gives "When you're approved, your first business starts with a 14-day free trial"; open gives "Start your 14-day free trial"; no trial in the catalog, or a closed door, gives no trial line. A test pins each mode.
- **Google Business Profile writes** are on in dev and OFF in production, so the beta copy ("changes reach Google only once Peakhour switches sending on") is correct.
- **Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads and Search Console** are live on dev but `coming_soon` in production. Production wins: the pages stay unpublished and the hubs say "coming soon".
- **Support channels and AI replies** (shared inbox, AI reply drafts, WhatsApp support assistant) are still `coming_soon` in both.

## e) The production release checklist

Plan section 7, made concrete. All of these are owner actions: project settings are owner-only.

1. **Env values, Production only.**
   - `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://www.peakhour.ai` (plan D1; today production names `https://peakhour.ai` in every canonical, in the sitemap and in robots.txt, and that host answers 307 to www). The preview env keeps the default, which is why dev canonicals say `https://peakhour.ai`: that is correct and is what the smoke suite expects.
   - **`SITE_INDEXABLE` needs no value.** The plan says to set it true, but the code (`src/lib/seo/indexable.ts`) decides from `VERCEL_ENV === "production"`; `SITE_INDEXABLE=false` is only a switch-off. Setting it true does nothing.
2. **Vercel 307 to 308.** Make `peakhour.ai` to `www.peakhour.ai` a 308 permanent redirect (domain settings). Every canonical today points at a temporary redirect.
3. **What the coming-soon gate means for crawlers** (decide before flipping). With `COMING_SOON=true` the sitemap lists only what the gate lets through: `/`, `/pricing`, `/peaks`, `/shopify` and everything under it, `/launch-partner`, `/auth` and the legal pages. Every other published registry page (the pillar hubs, `/how-it-works`, `/features/*`, `/integrations/*`, `/about`) answers the teaser under `noindex`, so it cannot rank. To index the full site, turn the gate off; to index the Shopify set first, leave it on.
4. **Search Console.** Verify `www.peakhour.ai`, submit `https://www.peakhour.ai/sitemap.xml` (an index of named sitemaps), and record a baseline (indexed pages, queries) before any URL changes.
5. **Smoke against production**, after the deploy and before submitting the sitemap:

   ```
   npx tsx scripts/seo/smoke.ts https://www.peakhour.ai --indexable=yes --canonical-origin=https://www.peakhour.ai [--gate=on]
   ```

   With `--indexable=yes` it checks that robots.txt names the sitemap, no page carries a blanket `noindex`, every sitemap URL answers 200 with a self-canonical and parseable JSON-LD, review pages 404, and the registry crawl (T03 to T07, T14, T15). Anything that fails blocks the release.
6. **Also before release:** re-run `check-catalog.ts` (above), settle decisions 3 to 5 in section c, and decide Shopify's status.
