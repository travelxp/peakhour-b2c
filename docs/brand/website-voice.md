# Peakhour website voice (v1, 2026-11-16)

This is the standard for every public page. It is drawn from the Daylight `/shopify` page, the best copy on the site, and from the owner's rules. The audit lane commits it to b2c as `docs/brand/website-voice.md`.

## Who reads it
A busy merchant or marketer in India, the UK, the USA, Canada, Australia or New Zealand:
- someone running a Shopify store, a D2C brand, a local business or an agency;
- often on a phone;
- often not a native English speaker;
- deciding in seconds whether Peakhour is for them.

## The voice
- **Plain and concrete.** Say what happens, to what, and where. Write "Answers shoppers from your catalogue, on your store and on WhatsApp", not "AI-powered omnichannel engagement".
- **The outcome first, then how.** The first sentence of every section says the result. Mechanics come second.
- **Short.** Headlines run 3 to 8 words. Sentences average under 18 words. One idea per sentence, and 2 to 3 sentences per paragraph at most.
- **Active voice; "you" for the merchant, "Peakhour" for the product.** Never "we leverage" or "our cutting-edge".
- **Confident but honest.** State what it does, then its limits, without hedging the true parts. Say "In beta" and "Coming soon" plainly. Trust is the product: "You approve every change", "Undo any change", "Nothing goes live without you".
- **Specific nouns.** Use Shopify, WhatsApp, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, Peaks and Peakhour Summit, never "platforms", "channels" or "solutions" in the abstract.
- **No filler or hype.** Never write:
  - "revolutionise", "supercharge", "unlock", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "game-changer";
  - "harness the power of", "take your business to the next level", "all-in-one solution", "AI-powered" as a crutch;
  - "Welcome to";
  - exclamation marks.
- **No fake proof.** No invented stats, customers, logos, ratings, testimonials or "trusted by". An illustration is labelled "Example" or "Sample store".
- **No old positioning.**
  - Not "five modules, one brain", not "Your Smart Autonomous Business", not "Five modules. One platform. One plan.", and not "the AI marketing platform".
  - The current line is `SITE.tagline`: "The AI business platform for growing brands".
  - The coming-soon teaser line is "Your AI team behind every channel you sell on."

## Mechanics
- **Headings are sentence case.** Italic serif accent words ("*One plan.*") are gone.
- **Lead with the answer.** The first paragraph on a page answers the page's question in one or two sentences, for answer engines and for skimmers.
- **CTA labels are verbs.** Use "Become a Launch Partner", "See pricing", "Sign in", "Install on Shopify" when available, or "Join the waitlist" while signups are closed, as the catalog decides. A label is never just "Learn more"; use "See how COD confirmation works" instead.
- **Prices are in Peaks.** Never put ₹, £ or $ beside a Peakhour job price. Plan prices billed through Shopify follow the existing /shopify pattern.
- **Name things exactly.**
  - "Peakhour Summit", with no ™; never "council".
  - Its seats are "The case for", "The case against", "Safety checks" and "Final call".
  - "Smart Rails", "Find my match", "Buy together and save", "Peaks", "Launch Partner".
- **Lists are parallel and scannable.** 3 to 6 items, each starting with a verb or a noun, consistently.
- **FAQs** are real questions a merchant asks, answered in 1 to 3 sentences, with the answer first.
- **Brand:** the logo and name are only the official artwork components, never typed as a label.

## Before and after examples
- **Before:** "Your whole business, waiting on the other side. Free to try."
  **After:** "Sign in to Peakhour. We'll email you a link: no password needed."
- **Before:** "Everything a business does online. One plan."
  **After:** "One plan for everything Peakhour does. Pay for the AI work you use, in Peaks."
- **Before:** "Have a question, feedback, or want to explore how Peakhour can work for your business? We'd love to hear from you."
  **After:** "Questions about Peakhour? Write to the right inbox and a person replies."
